**MOHAVE VALLEY, Ariz.** — The Mohave Valley Fire Department will hold its annual Halloween Carnival on Friday, Oct. 23, from 5 to 8 p.m. at the department, 1451 Willow Drive.

The carnival is geared to children ages 2 to 12 and features games, prizes, food, a cake walk and a junior firefighter challenge.

Proceeds benefit the department's Christmas for Kids program.

More information: Mohave Valley Fire Department, 1451 Willow Drive, Mohave Valley.
