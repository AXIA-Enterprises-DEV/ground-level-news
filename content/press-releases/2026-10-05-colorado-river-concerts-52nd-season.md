**BULLHEAD CITY, Ariz.** — Colorado River Concerts will open its 52nd season with "Best of Broadway: The Music of Andrew Lloyd Webber & More" on Thursday, Nov. 5, at 7 p.m. at the Mohave High School Auditorium, 2251 Highway 95.

The opener features countertenor Terry Barber, a former Metropolitan Opera soloist, performing songs from Andrew Lloyd Webber shows along with selections from "Hamilton" and "Les Misérables." He will be joined by Grace Field, a member of the Tony Award-winning group Broadway Inspirational Voices.

The rest of the season, all at 7 p.m. at the same auditorium, according to the organization's website:

- **Thursday, Jan. 21, 2027:** CeCe Teneal's Divas of Soul, celebrating the women of R&B and soul
- **Tuesday, Feb. 23, 2027:** Spinphony, a classical/rock electric string quartet
- **Wednesday, March 10, 2027:** Radio Recall, a throwback show of hits from the 1970s, '80s and '90s

Season tickets for all four shows are $100, and individual tickets are $35. Youth 18 and under pay $5 with a paying adult. Seating is open. Tickets can be bought in advance at crconcerts.com or at the door on concert nights, subject to availability; the box office opens at 6 p.m. Sponsorships start at $300.

More information: crconcerts.com.
