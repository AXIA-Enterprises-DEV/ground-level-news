**LAS VEGAS** — Early voting for the Nov. 3, 2026, General Election in Clark County starts Saturday, Oct. 17, and continues through Friday, Oct. 30, according to the official notice from Registrar of Voters Lorena Portillo.

Any registered Clark County voter can vote at any early voting site, and every site also accepts mail ballot drop-offs during its listed hours. Same-day registration is available for eligible residents with a Nevada driver's license, Nevada ID card, DMV interim document or tribal ID, as are same-day updates to an existing registration.

Most sites are in the Las Vegas Valley, but the county is also sending short-term early voting sites to outlying communities:

- **Boulder City Recreation Center**, 900 Arizona St.: Oct. 17-21, 9 a.m.-6 p.m.
- **Searchlight Community Center**, 200 Michael Wendell Way: Oct. 22, 9 a.m.-4 p.m.
- **Laughlin Library**, 2840 S. Needles Hwy.: Oct. 23-24, 10 a.m.-6 p.m.; Oct. 25, 1-5 p.m.
- **Mesquite Jimmie Hughes Campus**, 150 N. Yucca St.: Oct. 26-28, 9 a.m.-6 p.m.
- **Moapa Valley Community Center**, 320 N. Moapa Valley Blvd., Overton: Oct. 29, 9 a.m.-6 p.m.
- **Sun City Mesquite**, 1350 Flat Top Mesa Dr.: Oct. 30, 9 a.m.-6 p.m.

Voters who received a mail ballot but choose to vote in person must surrender it at the site, or sign an affirmation if they no longer have it. The county says sites and hours can change and posts updates online, along with an interactive map of nearby sites during early voting.

More information: Clark County Election Department, (702) 455-VOTE (8683), TTY 711; ClarkCountyNV.gov/vote.
