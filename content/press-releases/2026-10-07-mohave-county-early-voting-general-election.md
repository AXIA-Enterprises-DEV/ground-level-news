**KINGMAN, Ariz.** — Early voting for the Nov. 3, 2026, General Election began Oct. 7 in Mohave County, the same day the county started mailing early ballots to voters.

In-person early voting runs through Friday, Oct. 30, at three Mohave County Library branches, Monday through Friday from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. On Oct. 30 the sites stay open until 7 p.m. (The county noted the sites were closed Monday, Oct. 12.) The locations are:

- **Bullhead City:** 1170 Hancock Road
- **Lake Havasu City:** 1770 McCulloch Blvd. N.
- **Kingman:** 3269 N. Burbank St.

Voters returning a mail ballot should allow about seven days for U.S. Postal Service delivery. Ballots must be received by 7 p.m. on Election Day. Drop boxes are available inside each early voting site during early voting hours, and inside the Mohave County Administration Building, 700 W. Beale St. in Kingman, weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

On Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, polls are open from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. The county encourages voters to confirm their polling place ahead of time, using the "PCT." field on their voter ID card or by looking it up at my.arizona.vote.

More information: Mohave County Elections, (928) 753-0733; Voter Registration, (928) 753-0767 or VoterRegistration@mohave.gov; mohave.gov.
