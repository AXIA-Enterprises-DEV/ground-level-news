**HENDERSON, Nev.** — The Clark County Museum will host its Trick-or-Treat Fest on Friday and Saturday, Oct. 16 and 17, from 6 to 10 p.m., one of several Halloween events Clark County Parks & Recreation is offering this month.

Visitors trick-or-treat along the museum's Historic Heritage Street, a row of restored historic homes, with live entertainment, themed games and costume contests. The county's Halloween events page lists admission at $15. The museum is at 1830 S. Boulder Highway in Henderson.

The county's Halloween lineup also includes Haunt the Wetlands at Clark County Wetlands Park, a Hallow Harvest Festival with a trunk-or-treat at Bob Price Recreation Center, and Halloween parties at neighborhood recreation centers. Closer to the rural communities, the Moapa Valley Halloween Party is set for Monday, Oct. 26, from 4:30 to 7:30 p.m. at the North County Fairgrounds, and is free.

The county says admission and activity fees vary by location, and many events are free or charge a small fee for activity tickets.
