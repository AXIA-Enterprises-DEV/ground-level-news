**LAUGHLIN, Nev.** — The American Off-Road Racing Championship (AORC) has named NOFX frontman and Punk Rock Museum co-founder Fat Mike as Grand Marshal of the 2026 Laughlin Desert Classic, Oct. 15-18.

The race is the AORC season finale. Its multi-lap, short-course desert format lets spectators watch the cars come around again and again. Race week also includes the two-day Laughlin Off-Road Festival, Oct. 16-17, in downtown Laughlin, with race vehicles, aftermarket manufacturers, racers and fans.

Separately, UNLTD Off-Road Racing named Rugged Road the event's official cooler and will give five custom coolers to race fans ([The Parker 400](https://theparker400.com/news/rugged-road-joins-the-2026-laughlin-desert-classic-as-the-official-cooler/)).
