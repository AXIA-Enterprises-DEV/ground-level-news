**LAKE HAVASU CITY, Ariz.** — Lake Havasu City will begin sealing streets and park parking lots on Sunday, Oct. 18, as part of its pavement preservation program. The work is expected to take about a week.

Crews will apply a high-density mineral bond (HA5) surface treatment, which protects asphalt and extends the life of roads and parking lots. About 38,000 square yards will be treated citywide, including designated streets and several city park parking lots.

Because HA5 needs extra time to cure, drivers should expect delays, restrictions and temporary road and lane closures. The city asks motorists to follow traffic control, use alternate routes where possible, and stay off treated surfaces until traffic control devices are removed. Residents on scheduled streets will be notified before work begins and are asked not to park on affected streets and to avoid letting water run into the roadway during the work.

Each park parking lot will be closed to vehicles for about 24 hours, starting early in the morning and reopening the next morning, with signs posted in advance. The schedule, which may change with the weather:

- **Aquatic Center:** Sunday, Oct. 18
- **SARA Park Trailhead:** Monday, Oct. 19
- **Cypress Park South:** Wednesday, Oct. 21
- **Cypress Park East:** Thursday, Oct. 22

Maps and street lists are posted on the city's Streets page. More information: Lake Havasu City Public Works, (928) 855-3377; lhcaz.gov.
