**LAKE MEAD NATIONAL RECREATION AREA** — Lake Mead National Recreation Area has been named one of Leave No Trace's Spotlight locations for 2026, and the organization's traveling team is scheduled to be at the park Oct. 29-31.

As part of the visit, the team and other park partners will set up a free pop-up education station at the Arizona Hot Springs Trailhead on Friday, Oct. 30, from 8 a.m. to noon.

Hikers and visitors can stop by the tables to learn about staying safe on the trail, protecting public lands while recreating, and local stewardship and volunteer opportunities in the park.

More information: Lake Mead volunteer office, LAKE_Volunteer_Coordinator@nps.gov or 702-293-8711; nps.gov/lake.
